## Purpose

*What blind spot does a Product Leader catch that Intake or Analyse-Explore might not, left to themselves? Likely candidates to react to, not final answers: a Brief that's internally coherent but doesn't survive contact with "why should this beat the next three things on the roadmap"; an Executive Summary that describes activity instead of outcome; a Success Criteria section that's measurable but not tied to a business goal anyone senior actually holds.*
